The human layer

The Strategist is the judgement layer on top of the system.

A senior operator who owns the client relationship, picks the plays, writes the Playbook narrative, and is accountable for the Compound Score moving.

The OS handles the cadence, the data, the routing and the credits. The Strategist handles the calls the system can't make — which play to prioritise, when to escalate, when to kill a loser, how to frame a win.

Core: 1 Strategist · Growth: CMO + 1 Strategist · Performance: CMO + 2 Strategists
