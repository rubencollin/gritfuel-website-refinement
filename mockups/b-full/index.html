Common questions

What founders ask before
the first discovery call.

How is this different from hiring a fractional CMO?

A fractional CMO is one person on your calendar. GritFuel is a system with a CMO inside it. The Dashboard, the Playbook, the Escalation Router and the credit pool all keep running even when a single person isn't. You buy operating rhythm — not headcount.

What's the "credit pool" and how is pricing transparent?

Your monthly plan is split into two streams: a fixed subscription for the operating system, and a credit pool for the actual work. Credits resolve against a 153-item ALC (Agency Line Card) Menu — every line has a price, a deliverable, and the margin we keep. You always see what a credit buys.

What happens in the first 30 days?

Day 1–5: Onboarding Intake + scoping. Day 5: first Playbook signed off. Day 5–28: execution loop — three plays running, credits turning into deliverables, the Escalation Router active. Day 28: Compound Score in, next Playbook out. Then the month compounds.

What does "the Compound Score" actually measure?

Four signals weighted to your business: Demand, Conversion, Retention, Brand. Each is scored 0–100 monthly. The Compound Score is the weighted composite. It is designed to reward moves that still pay you back in six months — not vanity spikes.
